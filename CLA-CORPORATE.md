# Airclone Corporate Contributor Agreement

**Copyright assignment · Version 1.0**

## In plain words

This summary is here to help you read the agreement. It is not part of it.

This agreement is for a company or other organization whose employees or contractors contribute to
Airclone as part of their work. The organization, which usually owns what those people write,
transfers the copyright in their contributions to Gigaion, LLC, on the same terms each of them
accepts personally under the [individual agreement](CLA.md).

- The organization **transfers the copyright** in those contributions to Gigaion, and **gets a
  license back** to use them for anything it likes.
- Gigaion can publish the contributions on any terms, including closed source and through app
  stores.
- The organization lists the people who contribute on its behalf, and tells Gigaion when that list
  changes.
- **Each of those people still signs the individual agreement**, by posting a comment on their first
  pull request. The automated check looks for that comment, and this agreement does not replace it.

## The agreement

This Corporate Contributor Agreement (the "Agreement") is between Gigaion, LLC and the organization
named in the signature block below.

### 1. Definitions

- **"Corporation"** means the legal entity that signs this Agreement, together with its
  Affiliates.
- **"Affiliate"** means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with
  the signing entity, where "control" means owning more than 50% of the voting interests or
  otherwise having the power to direct its management.
- **"Designated Contributor"** means an individual listed in Schedule A, as the Corporation updates
  it under Section 5.
- **"Contribution"** means any Contribution, as defined in the Individual CLA, that a Designated
  Contributor Submits on the Corporation's behalf, or in which the Corporation holds any rights.
- **"Individual CLA"** means version 1.0 of the Airclone Contributor Agreement, published as CLA.md
  in the GigaionLLC/Airclone repository.
- "Gigaion", "Project", "Submit" and "Copyright" have the meanings the Individual CLA gives them.

### 2. Assignment and licenses

The Corporation makes the assignment in Section 2, and grants the licenses in Sections 3 and 7, of
the Individual CLA, for all Contributions. Read for this purpose, "You" in those Sections means the
Corporation, so the assignment covers every right the Corporation holds in the Contributions, and
the patent license covers every patent claim that the Corporation can license.

### 3. Terms adopted from the Individual CLA

Sections 4 (License back to You), 5 (Gigaion's freedom to use Contributions), 6 (Moral rights),
9 (Further assurances), 10 (No obligation), 11 (Disclaimer), 12 (Transfer), 13 (New versions) and
15 (General) of the Individual CLA apply to this Agreement. In them, "You" means the Corporation,
and "Your Contributions" means the Contributions as this Agreement defines them.

### 4. What the Corporation promises

The Corporation represents that:

- (a) it is legally entitled to make the assignment and grant the licenses in this Agreement, and
  the person signing it is authorized to bind the Corporation;
- (b) each Designated Contributor is authorized to Submit Contributions on the Corporation's
  behalf;
- (c) it has not assigned, exclusively licensed or otherwise transferred any right in the
  Contributions to anyone else, and will not; and
- (d) to the best of its knowledge, each Contribution is the original work of a Designated
  Contributor, apart from material identified as Section 8(e) of the Individual CLA requires, and
  does not infringe anyone's copyright, patent, trademark, trade secret or other rights.

### 5. Keeping Schedule A current

The Corporation will name a point of contact below. That person may add people to Schedule A, or
remove them, by written notice to Gigaion. The Corporation will remove a person promptly once they
are no longer authorized to contribute on its behalf. Removing someone does not affect
Contributions they Submitted before the notice.

## How to sign

Fill in the signature block and Schedule A, sign, and send the signed copy to Gigaion, LLC at
[billing@gigaion.com](mailto:billing@gigaion.com). Do not post it in an issue or a pull request:
the signed copy holds your contact details, and this repository is public.

Each Designated Contributor then signs the [individual agreement](CLA.md) by posting the signing
comment on their first pull request.
