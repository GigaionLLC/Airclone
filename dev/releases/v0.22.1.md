# Airclone v0.22.1 — hold to fast-forward

A follow-up for Google TV and Android TV, from the same person whose reports
shaped v0.22.0. Music was right; video skipping was not.

## Skipping through a video works, and holding keeps going

In v0.22.0, pressing the on-screen fast-forward or rewind button skipped once —
and then the next press paused the film and jumped back to the Play button, so
you could never skip twice in a row. That is fixed: the button you pressed stays
selected, and every press skips again.

And now you can **hold** it. Keep OK held on ⏪ or ⏩ (or hold the remote's own
rewind / fast-forward key) and the film keeps moving, faster the longer you
hold, until you let go. A quick press is still a 30-second jump.

Holding OK anywhere else is now a single press too, so a long press on
play/pause no longer flickers between playing and paused.

## Notes

- **TV-only behaviour, except the remote keys.** Holding a keyboard's or
  Bluetooth remote's rewind / fast-forward key scans on every platform, the same
  way it does on a television. No phone, tablet or desktop layout changes.
- **Windows builds are signed** (Azure Artifact Signing, OV-class — SmartScreen
  may still warn until the certificate builds reputation). macOS builds are
  signed and notarized with a Developer ID.
- **Verified by its test suite, not on a physical television.** The tests drive
  real key presses through the player's controls and were confirmed to fail on
  v0.22.0's behaviour. If it is still not right on your set, please say so.
