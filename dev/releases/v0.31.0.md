# Airclone v0.31.0 — minimize to the tray on Windows

## Minimizing can hide Airclone in the tray (#54)

Settings → Tray → **Minimizing the window hides it to the tray**. It's off by
default and only on Windows. Turn it on and the minimize button (or Win+Down)
hides the window instead of leaving it on the taskbar. Transfers, mounted
drives, servers and scheduled tasks keep running. Click the tray icon, choose
**Open Airclone** from its menu, or start Airclone again to bring the window
back.

It works on its own: you don't need **Closing the window keeps Airclone
running in the tray** or **Start Airclone in the tray when I sign in** turned
on. Without a tray icon the switch is greyed out and minimizing works as
before.

## Notes

- macOS, Linux, Android, iPhone, iPad and the Web UI are unchanged.

Full details: [Keeping Airclone in the tray](../../docs/guide/tray.md).
