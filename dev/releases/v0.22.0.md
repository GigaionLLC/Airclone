# Airclone v0.22.0 — the remote can run the player now

If you use Airclone on a Google TV or Android TV, this one is for you. Music and
video played fine; the remote just could not do much about either. Now it can.

## What a remote can do during playback

Press anything and the controls appear, sized for a television rather than a
phone. Then:

- **Left and right skip** backward and forward through a film — 10 seconds, and
  faster if you keep pressing, so getting across a two-hour film does not mean
  sixty presses.
- **OK pauses**, and brings the controls back when they have faded.
- **Back** clears the controls off the screen, and leaves the player next time.
- **Your remote's own buttons work** — play/pause, rewind, fast-forward, and
  next/previous track — wherever you happen to be on the screen.

The controls fade out while a film plays, so the picture is the picture. They do
**not** fade while it is paused, because a paused film with nothing on screen is
a film you cannot restart.

## Music got a screen built for a television

Previous and next have been there since v0.8.0, but on a TV they were two small
icons in a card built for a mouse, and the only way to reach them was to press
the arrow keys until something highlighted. That is not a reasonable thing to ask
of anyone sitting across a room.

The audio player on a television is now a full screen: large artwork, the track
name at a size you can read from a sofa, and a row of controls you can actually
aim at — with play/pause already selected the moment it opens, so OK works
straight away.

## Skipping tracks moves between tracks

Next and previous now move to the next file **of the same kind**. A folder of
songs almost always has a `cover.jpg` in it, and often a `.cue` as well; until
now, "next track" could drop you onto the picture halfway through an album. On a
phone that was a shrug — you swipe again. On a television those two buttons are
the only way through the folder, so it made an album unplayable end to end.

Videos behave the same way: the next episode, not the subtitle file sitting next
to it.

## Notes

- **The transport buttons work everywhere**, not only on a television — a
  keyboard's media keys and a Bluetooth remote paired to a phone are the same
  buttons, so they are honoured on every platform. Everything else in this
  release is TV-only, and no phone, tablet or desktop layout changes.
- **A live stream has nothing to seek within**, so on one the player says *Live*
  rather than offering a progress bar that cannot move.
- **Windows builds are signed** (Azure Artifact Signing, OV-class — SmartScreen
  may still warn until the certificate builds reputation). macOS builds are
  signed and notarized with a Developer ID.
- **This release was verified by its test suite, not on a physical television.**
  The behaviour is covered by tests that were each confirmed to fail without the
  fix, and the remote's transport keys were measured reaching the app on an
  Android TV emulator — but nobody here has a set to try it on. If something is
  off on yours, please say so; the last three TV problems were all found this
  way.
