# Airclone Contributor Agreement

**Copyright assignment · Version 1.0**

## In plain words

This summary is here to help you read the agreement. It is not part of it.

- **You transfer the copyright** in what you contribute to Gigaion, LLC, which publishes Airclone.
  From then on, Gigaion owns it.
- **You get a license back.** You can keep using your own contribution for anything you like,
  including in other projects and under any license.
- **Gigaion can publish your contribution on any terms:** the AGPLv3, another open-source license,
  a commercial license, closed source, or through app stores.
- **You promise it is yours to give**, or that your employer has agreed.
- **Versions of Airclone already released under the AGPLv3 stay licensed under the AGPLv3** for
  anyone who has a copy. That is how open-source licenses work, and nothing here changes it.
- **To sign**, post this as a comment on your pull request:
  `I have read the Airclone CLA and I hereby sign it.` You only do this once.

Why we ask for it is explained in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#the-contributor-agreement).

## How You accept this CLA

You accept this CLA, for all Your Contributions, by posting the following as a comment on a pull
request in the Project repository, from a GitHub account You control:

```
I have read the Airclone CLA and I hereby sign it.
```

That comment is Your electronic signature. Gigaion keeps a record of it on the `cla-signatures`
branch of the repository: Your GitHub username and numeric account ID, the pull request and
comment, the time You posted it, the version of this CLA, and a permanent link to the exact text You
signed. Like the comment itself, that record is public.
